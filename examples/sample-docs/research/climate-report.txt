Climate Adaptation in Coastal Cities

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change warns that sea levels could rise by up to one meter by 2100. Coastal cities such as Miami, Jakarta, and Rotterdam face increasing flood risk.

Rotterdam has invested heavily in water plazas, storm surge barriers, and floating neighborhoods. The Maeslantkering barrier, completed in 1997, protects the port from North Sea storm surges.

Jakarta is sinking faster than almost any other city because of groundwater extraction. The Indonesian government announced plans to move the capital to Nusantara on the island of Borneo.

Miami Beach has raised roads and installed pumps, but researchers at Florida International University caution that porous limestone limits the effectiveness of sea walls.
