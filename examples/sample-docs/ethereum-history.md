# A Short History of Ethereum

Ethereum was proposed in late 2013 by **Vitalik Buterin**, a programmer who had previously co-founded Bitcoin Magazine. The network went live on July 30, 2015.

## The Ethereum Foundation

The Ethereum Foundation, a non-profit based in Zug, Switzerland, supports development of the protocol. Early contributors included Gavin Wood, who wrote the [Yellow Paper](https://ethereum.github.io/yellowpaper/paper.pdf), and Joseph Lubin, who later founded ConsenSys.

## The DAO incident

In June 2016, an attacker exploited a vulnerability in The DAO smart contract and drained roughly 3.6 million ether. The community responded with a contentious hard fork, which split the chain into Ethereum and Ethereum Classic.

## The Merge

In September 2022, Ethereum completed The Merge, switching from proof-of-work mining to proof-of-stake consensus. The transition reduced the network's energy consumption by more than 99 percent.
