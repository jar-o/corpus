# Bitcoin Overview

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency introduced in 2008 by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto. The first block, known as the genesis block, was mined on January 3, 2009.

Bitcoin relies on proof-of-work mining, in which miners compete to solve cryptographic puzzles. Critics, including researchers at the University of Cambridge, have highlighted the energy consumption of mining.

- Supply is capped at 21 million coins.
- Blocks are produced roughly every ten minutes.
- The Lightning Network enables faster off-chain payments.

Bitcoin Magazine, co-founded by Vitalik Buterin, was one of the earliest publications covering the currency.
